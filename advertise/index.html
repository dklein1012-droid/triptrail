Questions

How much does it cost?

Flat placements are $99, $199, or $299 a month depending on reach. Pay per click starts at $0.60 per valid click with a monthly cap you set. There is no setup fee.

How does billing work?

We invoice monthly. For pay per click, you are billed only for valid clicks, only up to your cap. When the cap is reached, billing stops for the month.

What counts as a valid click?

A real traveler clicking through to your business. Bots are filtered before billing, repeat clicks inside the protection window are charged once at most, your own team’s visits are excluded, and every billed click is recorded in a signed audit trail.

How do I start?

Email hello@gettriptrail.com with your destination and the placement you are interested in. We reply within 2 business days with availability, exact placement options, and a one page agreement.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Placements run month to month with no long term contract. Just let us know before your next month starts.

Ready to reach trip planners?

Tell us which destination your business serves, and we will take it from there. No setup fee, and we reply within 2 business days.
