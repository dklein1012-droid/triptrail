About Poconos, PA

The Poconos pack a full family weekend into one compact mountain region: the 'Niagara of Pennsylvania' at Bushkill Falls, the slides and rides of Camelbeach, and the quiet trails of the Delaware Water Gap. Everything on this itinerary sits within a 30-minute drive of Stroudsburg, so you spend the weekend doing things, not driving.

Poconos, PA guide

The Pocono Mountains have been the Northeast's easy escape for generations, a forested plateau of lakes, waterfalls, and resort towns about two hours from both New York and Philadelphia. The landscape is the draw: the Delaware River carving its gap through the ridge, hemlock-shaded trails ending at waterfalls, and small towns like Stroudsburg and Jim Thorpe filling in the evenings. This weekend pairs the region's best natural sights with one big day of pure fun.

Water is the theme. Bushkill Falls threads boardwalks and stairs past eight waterfalls in a steep green gorge, while Dingmans Falls in the Delaware Water Gap lets you walk an accessible boardwalk to one of the tallest waterfalls in Pennsylvania. Balance the hiking with Camelbeach, where the waterpark spreads across the ski slopes of Camelback Mountain, and the mountain coaster and zipline handle the adrenaline for older kids. Keep the driving loops short, because Pocono sights scatter across the plateau and the winding roads between them are part of the scenery.

The Poconos suit families who want an outdoors-first weekend with a waterpark safety net, and the trip works from late spring through fall color. Pack layers even in summer, since the mountain air cools fast after sunset, and bring water shoes for creek-side exploring. Families with toddlers should plan waterfall visits around nap schedules and carriers rather than strollers, as the gorge trails involve plenty of stairs.

Attractions in Poconos, PA

Bushkill Falls

Day 1 · Morning · 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM · $20/adult, $11/child (est.)

Arrive right at opening to beat the crowds on the red trail, which passes all eight waterfalls including the 100-foot main falls. The boardwalk-and-stairs loop takes about 2 hours at a family pace. Pack a picnic — the grounds have shaded tables near the entrance.

Camelbeach Mountain Waterpark

Day 1 · Afternoon · 1:00 PM – 5:30 PM · $45/person (est.)

Drive about 30 minutes via US-209 to Camelbeach in Tannersville. Spend the afternoon on the water slides and wave pool; the Kahuna Lagoon area is ideal for younger kids. Dry rides (mountain coaster access is separate) round out the day if energy remains.

Downtown Stroudsburg

Day 1 · Evening · 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM · $60/family dinner (est.)

Head 20 minutes east to Stroudsburg for dinner on Main Street, then an easy evening stroll and ice cream. Early night — day two starts on the trail.

Dingmans Falls, Delaware Water Gap

Day 2 · Morning · 9:00 AM – 11:30 AM · Free (est.)

Drive 25 minutes north to Dingmans Falls, the second-tallest waterfall in Pennsylvania. The boardwalk to the falls is flat, stroller-friendly, and only about half a mile round trip — a gentle contrast to yesterday's stairs. Silverthread Falls is on the same trail.

Camelback Mountain Adventures

Day 2 · Afternoon · 12:30 PM – 4:00 PM · $35–$55/person for activities (est.)

Drive 35 minutes south to Camelback for the mountain coaster — riders control their own speed, and kids can ride tandem with an adult. The 4,000-foot zipline and treetop course are there for older kids and teens wanting more adrenaline.

Barley Creek Brewing Company, Tannersville

Day 2 · Evening · 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM · $70/family dinner (est.)

Finish the weekend 10 minutes away in Tannersville with a relaxed family dinner — the brewpub has a big kids' menu and outdoor seating in summer. Easy hop onto I-80 for the drive home.
