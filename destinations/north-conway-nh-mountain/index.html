About North Conway, NH

North Conway is the White Mountains' family headquarters — vintage trains, the Cog Railway up Mount Washington, and Story Land for little kids, all within 20 minutes. The Kancamagus Highway is one of America's great scenic drives. Summer and fall are peak; winter brings skiing at nearby Cranmore and Attitash. North Conway, NH guide

North Conway is the White Mountains' classic basecamp, a village of outlets, inns, and ice cream stands sitting in the Mount Washington Valley with rock ledges rising on both sides. Skiers founded its reputation, but summer and fall bring just as many people for the trains, rivers, and trailheads. Everything in this trip radiates from the village: storybook rides for little kids, a vintage railroad, and the tallest peak in the Northeast looming over it all.

The signature experience is Mount Washington itself. The Cog Railway has climbed the mountain's western slope on a rack-and-pinion track since 1869, and the summit, famous for some of the most extreme weather ever recorded, can swing from sunshine to near-freezing wind while the valley basks below. Balance the mountain days with the Conway Scenic Railroad's valley excursions, Story Land's gentle storybook rides built for the under-eight crowd, and Diana's Baths, where a short forest walk leads to cascades and pools perfect for cautious splashing. The Kancamagus Highway, the region's great scenic drive, links it all to the Pemigewasset wilderness.

North Conway suits families squarely, with the best range for ages four and up, and it shines from June through foliage season. Two rules for Washington: check the summit forecast before you go, and pack real layers no matter how warm the village feels, because the top is another climate entirely. Book Cog Railway seats ahead in summer and fall. If a day turns wet, the village's outlets and the railroad's covered cars keep the trip on track.

Attractions in North Conway, NH

Conway Scenic Railroad

~$25/adult, $18/child coach (est.)

Vintage trains wind through the White Mountains on several routes — the 1-hour Bartlett trip is perfect for little kids, the 5-hour Crawford Notch ride wows everyone. The 1874 Victorian station in North Conway is half the fun.

Mount Washington Cog Railway

~$79/adult, $45/child (est.)

The world's first mountain-climbing cog railway, chugging up Mount Washington since 1869. The 3-hour round trip to the 6,288-foot summit is a genuine marvel — bring jackets, it's 30 degrees colder on top. Book weeks ahead.

Story Land

~$44/person (est.)

New England's beloved storybook theme park in nearby Glen, built for kids 2–12. Gentle rides, costumed characters, and the antique cars — plus Cinderella's castle. Go on a weekday to skip the lines.

Diana's Baths

~$5/vehicle day pass (est.)

A series of cascading waterfalls and natural pools in Bartlett, reached by an easy 0.6-mile trail. Kids splash in the potholes and slide down the smooth rock chutes in summer. The Forest Service parking lot requires a day pass.
