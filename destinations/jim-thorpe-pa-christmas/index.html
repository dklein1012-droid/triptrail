About Jim Thorpe, PA

Jim Thorpe packs a full Christmas season into one walkable Victorian downtown: a Santa train through the Lehigh Gorge, Olde Time Christmas weekends with a tree lighting and carolers, a Gilded Age mansion on the hill, and the Old Jail of the Molly Maguires. This two-day plan is anchored downtown, about two hours from New York City. All prices and hours below are estimates — verify before you go, as holiday dates and hours are set by season.

Jim Thorpe, PA guide

Every December, Jim Thorpe turns its Victorian downtown into a Christmas card. The old coal town of Mauch Chunk, tucked into a mountain gap on the Lehigh River, strings lights over Broadway, and the season runs from the Santa trains the weekend after Thanksgiving through the Olde Time Christmas weekends in December. It is about two hours from New York City, and at Christmas it feels like a mountain village from another century.

A good Jim Thorpe weekend runs on a simple rhythm: train by day, lights after dark. Start with the Santa Claus Special, an 80-minute ride into the Lehigh Gorge with Santa and his elves visiting every car, then walk Broadway for the Olde Time Christmas weekends: carolers, a scavenger hunt, horse-and-carriage rides, and shops in Victorian storefronts. Day two belongs to the town's history: the Asa Packer Mansion, an 1861 Italianate villa frozen in the Gilded Age, and the Old Jail Museum, where the Molly Maguires awaited their fate. The Mauch Chunk Museum's Winter Wonderland village is the warm indoor finish.

Jim Thorpe suits families with kids four and up best, and it is at its best on the Olde Time Christmas weekends of December 5–6, 12–13, and 19–20, when the mansion, the museum village, and the downtown events all overlap. Book the Santa train online before you go; Christmas dates sell out. One honest warning: an October 31 rockslide closed Route 209 and moved the train's boarding to nearby Nesquehoning, so check lgsry.com the week of your ride. Dress warm, arrive early for parking, and save dinner for Broadway, where the restaurants sit in the same Victorian buildings as the shops.

Attractions in Jim Thorpe, PA

Lehigh Gorge Scenic Railway

~$25/adult, $9/child 3–12 (est.)

The Santa Claus Specials run November 27 to December 24, with departures at 10:30 AM, 1 PM, and 3:30 PM. The 80-minute round trip winds through Lehigh Gorge State Park while Santa and his elves visit every car with small treats. One warning: after an October 31 rockslide, trains have been boarding in nearby Nesquehoning instead of downtown Jim Thorpe, so check lgsry.com before your ride.

Olde Time Christmas

Free to wander

Three December weekends (Dec 5–6, 12–13, 19–20) turn Broadway into a Victorian Christmas: a tree lighting on December 4, carolers, horse-and-carriage rides, a scavenger hunt through the shops, story time with Mrs. Claus, and Nativity displays at St. John's. The celebration itself is free; the train, food, and parking cost extra.

Asa Packer Mansion

~$20/adult (est.)

The 1861 Italianate villa of railroad magnate Asa Packer, founder of Lehigh University: 18 rooms and about 11,000 square feet, preserved with its original Victorian furnishings. Guided tours run about 45 minutes. In November and December the mansion opens weekends only, the first two weekends of December, weather permitting.

Old Jail Museum

~$13/adult (est.)

The 1870 Carbon County Jail, where the Molly Maguires awaited trial and execution in the 1870s. Guided tours visit the cell block, the dungeon, the recreated gallows, and Cell 17, where a handprint is said to have defied every attempt to remove it. Open November weekends and into early December, weather permitting; tickets are sold in person only.
