About Hershey, PA

Three days in Hershey is the platonic ideal of the family vacation: one full day at Hersheypark's coasters and waterpark, one day going deep on chocolate at Chocolate World and the Hershey Story, and a third day for ZooAmerica's wildlife and the underground world of Indian Echo Caverns. Everything is 15 minutes apart, and the whole town smells faintly of cocoa.

Hershey, PA guide

Hershey is the rare company town that became a destination in its own right, built by Milton Hershey around his chocolate factory and still scented faintly of cocoa near the plant. Everything here connects back to that story: the streetlights shaped like Kisses, the free tour ride at Chocolate World that explains how a cacao bean becomes a candy bar, and a major amusement park that grew out of a picnic ground Hershey built for his workers. Three days lets you do it all without mainlining sugar from dawn to dusk.

Split the trip between park and town. Hersheypark anchors the visit with a full coaster lineup, a waterpark inside the gates, and ZooAmerica's collection of North American animals right next door. Then slow down at Chocolate World, where you can ride the tour, make your own candy bar, and catch the trolley tour that tells Milton Hershey's genuinely remarkable story of failure, fortune, and philanthropy. If your crew needs a break from the sweet theme entirely, Indian Echo Caverns offers a cool underground reset a short drive away.

Hershey suits families across the board, with toddlers entertained at Chocolate World and teens busy on the big coasters. Visit on weekdays in May, June, or September to dodge the peak summer lines, and check height requirements before promising anyone a specific ride, since the major coasters gate at taller heights. Build in one unstructured evening, because the town itself, with its gardens, outlets, and chocolate-scented air, is half the fun.

Attractions in Hershey, PA

Hersheypark

Day 1 · Morning · 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM · $85/person (est.)

Arrive at rope drop and head straight for the coasters — Candymonium, Skyrush, and Wildcat's Revenge build the longest lines by noon. Families with small kids should start in the Kissing Tower Hill and Founder's Way areas, which cluster the gentler rides together.

The Boardwalk at Hersheypark

Day 1 · Afternoon · 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM · Included with park ticket (est.)

Cool off at The Boardwalk, Hersheypark's waterpark-within-the-park — the Shore wave pool and Breakers Edge water coaster are the headliners. Change at the bathhouses and ride the dry coasters again as lines thin in late afternoon.

The Chocolatier Restaurant

Day 1 · Evening · 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM · $75/family dinner (est.)

Dinner at The Chocolatier, the restaurant just outside the park gates — chocolate-themed dishes that are genuinely good, not gimmicky. If legs allow, re-enter the park for night rides on Candymonium with the lights on.

Hershey's Chocolate World

Day 2 · Morning · 9:00 AM – 12:30 PM · Free tour ride; experiences $15–$35 (est.)

Start at Hershey's Chocolate World with the free chocolate factory tour ride — a slow-moving classic the whole family rides together. Book the Create Your Own Candy Bar experience in advance; morning slots sell out on summer weekends.

The Hershey Story Museum and Hershey Gardens

Day 2 · Afternoon · 1:30 PM – 5:00 PM · $15/adult, $12/child (est.)

Walk next door to The Hershey Story Museum for the interactive exhibits on Milton Hershey's life, then stroll the 23-acre Hershey Gardens — the rose garden peaks in June and the butterfly atrium runs seasonally. Both are an easy 10-minute walk from Chocolate World.

The Mill in Hershey

Day 2 · Evening · 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM · $110/family dinner (est.)

Dinner at The Mill in Hershey, the town's upscale-but-welcoming chophouse in a restored 19th-century gristmill. Afterward, drive Chocolate Avenue slowly — the Hershey's Kisses streetlamps are worth the photo.

ZooAmerica North American Wildlife Park

Day 3 · Morning · 9:00 AM – 10:30 AM · Included with Hersheypark ticket (est.)

Use the second-day admission perk: ZooAmerica, the 11-acre North American wildlife park, is included with your Hersheypark ticket and opens early. The nocturnal hall and the prairie dog town are kid favorites; plan about 90 minutes before checkout.

Indian Echo Caverns

Day 3 · Afternoon · 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM · $22/adult, $16/child (est.)

Drive 15 minutes to Hummelstown for the guided 45-minute tour of Indian Echo Caverns — a constant 52°F underground, so bring a layer even in August. The stalactite rooms and underground lake are a cool contrast to two days of theme-park sun.

Downtown Hershey

Day 3 · Evening · 3:30 PM – 5:00 PM · $40/family (est.)

Back on Chocolate Avenue for a farewell round of ice cream and fudge, then hit the road — you're 90 minutes from Philadelphia and 2 hours from NYC with full chocolate cargo.
