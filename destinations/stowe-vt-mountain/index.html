About Stowe, VT

Stowe is Vermont's classic mountain town — a storybook village at the foot of Mount Mansfield, the state's highest peak. Summer brings gondola rides, the Ben & Jerry's factory tour, and the famous recreation path; winter brings top-tier skiing. Fall foliage here is among the best in New England. Stowe, VT guide

Stowe is the Vermont mountain town that appears on postcards: a white-steepled church, a main street of inns and general stores, and Mount Mansfield, the state's highest peak, rising behind it all. In winter it is one of the East's premier ski towns. In summer it trades the slopes for gondola rides, waterfall swims, and bike paths, with the same storybook village at the center. This trip works in both seasons, which is rare and worth planning around.

The mountain anchors everything. Ride the gondola up Mansfield for summit views and the cliff walk, or drive the historic toll road if it is open. In the village orbit sit Vermont's most delicious stops: the Ben and Jerry's factory in nearby Waterbury, where the tour ends with the flavor of the day, and Cold Hollow Cider Mill, where the cider donuts come warm and the old press still runs. The von Trapp family's lodge adds a Sound of Music chapter, with mountain views from the meadow where the family settled after fleeing Austria.

Stowe suits outdoorsy families and couples, with the easiest wins for ages five and up. The flat, paved Recreation Path runs for miles along the river and handles strollers, new bike riders, and evening walks equally well. Summer visitors should sandwich in a swimming hole or waterfall afternoon, while winter visitors should book ski lessons and mountain dining ahead. Either way, save an unhurried hour for the village itself, which is small enough to know by heart in a weekend.

Attractions in Stowe, VT

Stowe Mountain Resort Gondola

~$38/adult, $26/child summer gondola (est.)

Ride the gondola to the top of Vermont's highest peak for sweeping views of the Green Mountains and Lake Champlain. In summer, the summit has easy trails and the Cliff House restaurant; in winter it's a top ski resort.

Ben & Jerry's Factory Tour

~$8/person tour (est.)

The 30-minute guided tour of the original factory in nearby Waterbury ends with a generous sample — the Flavor Graveyard of retired flavors is a kid favorite. Book tickets online well ahead; tours sell out daily in summer.

Trapp Family Lodge

Free to visit grounds; activities extra (est.)

The von Trapp family's famous mountainside lodge, with 2,600 acres of trails, farm animals, and Austrian-style architecture. Non-guests can hike, visit the bakery, and tour the grounds — the views alone are worth the drive up.

Cold Hollow Cider Mill

Free entry; donuts ~$2 each (est.)

A classic Vermont cider mill in Waterbury Center with free samples, legendary cider donuts, and a viewing window into the press room. The gift shop is dangerously good. A perfect 45-minute stop.
